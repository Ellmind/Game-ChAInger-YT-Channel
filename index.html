What the toolkit does

From a local plan to a YouTube update

Organize the catalog

Read channel, video, playlist, and public statistics data to build local reports and review the channel’s content.

Prepare and publish

Upload the owner’s original video files, set titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails, and apply approved visibility and publication schedules.

Maintain the series

Assign videos to relevant playlists and publish comments from plans reviewed and authorized by the channel owner.

Google authorization

Access is tied to a clear task

YMonstr uses the YouTube Data API v3. The desktop workflow requests https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl to read channel information and carry out supported write operations. Google’s consent screen describes the full access granted by this permission; it is broader than any individual task.

The current workflow checks the selected channel against the GameChAInger channel ID before a write operation. Uploads, metadata changes, playlist updates, and comments are carried out under the owner’s instructions, including approved batch plans and schedules.

Google sign-in happens on Google’s authorization page opened by the desktop toolkit. This information website has no sign-in form and does not collect Google passwords or OAuth tokens.

The current toolkit does not request Gmail, Google Drive, Contacts, YouTube Analytics API, or YouTube Reporting API access.

Review, revoke, or ask for help

Read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service before authorizing the app. To stop future Google API access, remove YMonstr in your Google Account connections. To request deletion of stored app data, email maratgood@gmail.com.

Using YMonstr also means agreeing to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google’s handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
